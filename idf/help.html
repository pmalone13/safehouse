Overview

Ideal Federal Technologies is a US corporation with standard Eastern time business operations. IFT monitors our operating environment 7x24, and we react immediately to any and all adverse events. IFT also monitors customer issues and troubles and we are notified immediately when clients submit issues. Please use the following guide for our expected response times.

  • Highest - Service unavailable: IFT will respond within 30 minutes
  • High - A work-around exists but problematic: IFT will respond within 2 hours during business operations
  • Medium - A work-around is OK but still a bug: IFT will respond within 5 business days
  • Low - Feature Request: IFT will respond within 5 business days
  • Lowest - Information Request: IFT will respond within 5 business days
Our clients are extremely important to us, please don't hesitate to open issues with us for any reason.
